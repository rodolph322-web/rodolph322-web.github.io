Comet Hop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What the game collects

Comet Hop does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score, your stardust, the comet skins you own and your sound setting on your own device so they are there next time you play. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet use

The first time the game opens, it downloads its fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your device's IP address as part of that request, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. After that, the game works offline.

Children

Comet Hop is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the Comet Hop page in the Google Play Store.
